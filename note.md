A note can now link a recording from `land record`: when `land.toml` names a `feed`, `land` lists the recording in the landing, links it by its feed address, and uploads it after the push. What fails to upload waits in an outbox that every later `land` sends first, and `land event` pushes a review finding or repair for the feed to show. `land init` writes the `feed` key and prints the Attn Land App's install link.
